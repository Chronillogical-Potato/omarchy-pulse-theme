# Container aquarium

A fish for every container of your `docker` panel: green when it is up, amber when it is unhealthy or restarting, red
when it stopped. Bubbles rise faster with your network. By Marco Porro, MIT.

**Needs** the [docker community source](../../docker/) (a panel called `docker`). Another panel with rows works too
(Proxmox VMs, UniFi clients...): *Install & edit*, select the fish, and pick it in *repeat → panel*.

**Actions**: a container goes down → the glass flashes red and lightning strikes · they are all up again → a shooting
star. Click a fish: its name and state.

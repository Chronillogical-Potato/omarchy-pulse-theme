# Green build rocket

A rocket of your own on its launch pad. By Marco Porro, MIT.

| What | Follows |
|---|---|
| the light on the tower | green, red while a build is failing (CI failing) |
| the smoke | your AI agents at work |
| the stars | come out when the Sun goes down |

**Actions**: CI passes again → the rocket lifts off, with Pulse's own launch · CI fails → it flashes red · click it →
it lifts off. Works with the Git and CI of your repos ([GUIDE.md](../../../docs/GUIDE.md)), no panel needed.

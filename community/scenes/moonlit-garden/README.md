# Moonlit garden

A night garden that follows your sky and your code. By Marco Porro, MIT.

| What | Follows |
|---|---|
| a flower for each repo | its colour is its CI, its size the commits of the week |
| the fireflies | your AI agents at work |
| the Moon | as bright as the real one tonight |
| the stars | more as the Sun goes down |

**Actions**: fireworks at midnight · a shooting star when the ISS passes over you · a flower flashes at every commit.
Click the Moon or a flower to read it.
